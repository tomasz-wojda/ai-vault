# Further Reading

These primary references inform the workflow. Follow the current official documentation for the tools and platforms actually used by the organization.

- Google SRE, **Managing Incidents** — incident roles, live incident state, communication, and handoff: https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/
- Google SRE, **Emergency Response** — response principles and recovery practices: https://sre.google/sre-book/emergency-response/
- Google SRE Workbook, **Incident Response** — worked incident investigation and the distinction between mitigation and complete root-cause discovery: https://sre.google/workbook/incident-response/
- Google SRE Workbook, **Postmortem Culture** — blameless analysis, preventive actions, and postmortem templates: https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/
- AWS Well-Architected Framework, **Responding to Events** — event processes, business impact, escalation, and recovery: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/responding-to-events.html
- OpenTelemetry, **Signals** — metrics, logs, traces, and related telemetry concepts: https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/
- OpenTelemetry, **Context Propagation** — correlating traces and logs across service/process boundaries: https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/context-propagation/
